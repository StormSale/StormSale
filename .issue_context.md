### User Story
As an advertiser organization with multiple financial managers, I want large escrow deposits and balance clawbacks to require multi-sig threshold approvals before transaction submission.

### Technical Specification
- Inspect account signers and thresholds via Horizon `/accounts/{address}`.
- Provide partial transaction XDR export/import modal for co-signers.
- Integrate SEP-0030 recovery and multi-party coordination protocol.

### Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Accounts with multi-sig thresholds display signing progress indicators (e.g., 1/2 signatures).
- [ ] Unsigned/partially signed XDRs can be copied or shared with team members.
- [ ] Full submission triggers automatically once threshold weight is satisfied.
