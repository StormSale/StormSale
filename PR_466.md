## Summary

Adds test coverage and a small, dependency-free payment-split utility for multi-asset batch sales, as requested in issue #466.

Sales paid in USDC, XLM or EURC split revenue between the creator, the platform fee and the affiliate pool. Previously the repository had stroop-exact splitting for affiliate auto-payouts and a DEX quote engine, but no single place that models the three-way sale split with basis points and guaranteed dust handling.

### What changed

- **`src/lib/multiAssetSplit.ts` (new)**
  - `PaymentSplitBps` / `validatePaymentSplit`: integer basis-point allocations that must sum to exactly `10_000` bps (100%), with validation of range and fractional values.
  - `splitSalePayment`: exact bigint (stroop / cent) arithmetic. Creator and affiliate-pool shares are floored independently; every indivisible remainder base unit is credited to the platform fee, so the allocations always sum to the original amount with no loss or minting.
  - `fetchMultiAssetQuote` / `quoteAndSplitSalePayment`: dynamic, injected-provider quote fetching for USDC/XLM/EURC conversion, then applies the split to the freshly quoted output.

- **`src/tests/multi-asset-split.test.ts` (new)** — 15 tests covering all acceptance criteria:
  - bps splits summing to exactly 10,000 (and rejection of 9,999 / 10,001, fractional, negative and out-of-range values);
  - dust handling: 1-stroop and awkward-cent remainders go to the platform fee; invariants verified over 375 amount/split combinations;
  - dynamic quotes for USDC→XLM, XLM→EURC and EURC→USDC with a mocked provider, including fresh-rate (no stale cache) verification and dust routing on quoted output.

No existing production code or tests were modified.
